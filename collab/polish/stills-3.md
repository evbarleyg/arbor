## Three rulings of the judge's, for the product owner to uphold or not

1. **A miss stands until a picture shows it cured.** A cell is as good as its worst kind of words; a kind that missed and is in no newer picture keeps the cell a miss, whatever has landed since. (The second round let two such misses go unseen without a word; `stills-2.md` says so now.)
2. **A legend that alone says what the signs mean is a word one must read to act**, as a placeholder that is its field's only label is: 4.5.
3. **A selected row in macOS's own colours is macOS's, whoever fills it**: white on the system's colour for a selection, nothing thinned, is what macOS's own lists show with Increase Contrast, and macOS alone is the bar. So 4.58 passes S6. The foot of the shortcuts' sheet, "Press any key to close" (4.09 in the dark look over white), is secondary: any key closes it unread.

**A question.** The coach and Check Setup are ordinary windows with an opaque ground: the same colour to the level over whatever lies behind, with the glass on as with it off. Their four cells "on glass" can never be seen, and count as missed for good. The rubric asks no cell "where a panel has no ground to see through". Strike the four (206 becomes 194)? They are counted until that is ruled.
