# The morning of 2026-10-15, in five lines

Asked for in `collab/HANDOFF-2026-10-14-maintainer.md`.

1. **What changed.** About 130 pull requests went onto the trunk since the handoff (98 were on it then, 230 now). Of the notes of first use, six are in (I16 keys that reached another app, I17 the list of windows, I19 the double-click, I20 c docking moves nothing else, I21, I25 d); two are approved and seen on a hosted Mac but not yet gated with the rest (I22 nine keys; I20 d and e with I23, nothing drawn unasked); three are built and not yet judged whole (I18, I20 a no Desktop column, I20 b a plain drag only moves).
2. **The numbers against macOS alone.** With nothing docked, 36 of a stranger's 1,000 acts go otherwise than without Arbor, on Build 1 and on the trunk of the small hours alike (not measured again since this morning's landings); nearly all of them are one fault (⌘M brings another app forward), and with its cure, which is approved but not in yet, it is 1 to 3. Keys, clicks and waiting cost the same with and without Arbor (simulated, counted once; on hosted Macs the seconds are alike).
3. **What is still rough.** Until the next line or two land: a plain drag still docks, fifty keys, the ring and the motion, ⌘M, a terminal that twitches. Not cured at all: two docks that cannot both fit still send the first out of view; a floating window still goes out of view with the view; on two displays of different sizes a docked window can end short of its place; with the Dock at the right, windows that are out of view show a corner.
4. **What was not measured.** The newcomer's eval (I24) did not run: its rig is built and rehearsed, but a scored run takes a go-ahead that is the maintainer's to give, and nobody asks at night. No person has used any of this, and none of the fourteen features that are on by default has been played on a hosted Mac on this morning's trunk.
5. **Is a build ready for a friend? Not yet.** `main` is worth building for whoever tried Build 1: better in everything that was played, worse in nothing we know of; `docs/RELEASE-NOTES.md` says at its head what it is and is not. A friend should wait for the second build. The public release stays the maintainer's decision.
