# The newcomer's eval (I24): the rig is up, nobody has played it yet

2026-10-15, 06:00 UTC. For the product owner, the coordinator and the maintainer's morning summary.

**In five lines.** The rig exists and works on GitHub's Macs: a picture and an act a round through Git, 6 to 8 seconds a round, no key, no call to GitHub's API
(`scripts/newcomer/README.md`, `make newcomer`). **There is no score.** A newcomer has to push its acts to this repository, and the permission check under which the agents work refused
that to a session that knows nothing, then refused a relay, then a helper inside the rig keeper's session: three times rightly, since nobody responsible had said it may.
It takes one sentence of the maintainer's: *a session that knows nothing about Arbor may push its acts to `mac-shell-out/newcomer.<run>.action`.*
With it: three runs, about four hours on one Mac, and the table Build 1 | the light Arbor | macOS alone.

## What was seen meanwhile

Two trials on hosted Macs (macOS 26.6, 1600x900), **played by the rig's author, who knows the way: they prove the instrument and are no score.** What the instrument itself measured holds whoever plays:

| | |
|---|---|
| Build 1 (`3deb36a`), one `⌃⌥⌘←` with six windows open, none docked | the window docked, and **five floating windows moved that nobody had touched**: moved untouched 5, unasked 5 (docs/VIABLE.md, section 6: "no floating window, ever") |
| A stranger's download of a build signed ad hoc, by somebody who knows every step | **17 acts** from the zip to the dialog that wants an administrator's password, which is the 18th: unpack, open, "Arbor Not Opened", Done, System Settings, Privacy & Security, down a long page (eight turns of the wheel), Open Anyway, Open Anyway. A lower bound: a stranger has to find that road first, and nothing on the way names it |

| macOS alone, one window fills the screen and stays | a double-click on the title bar; it stayed |

| macOS's own shortcuts (`fn` `⌃` `F` and the like) | answer posted events, so a newcomer can use what the menus show |

## What a round costs

Picture 0.2 s, push 1.5, fetch 0.5, act 0.8, windows at rest about 1; from the newcomer's word to the next picture in its hands 6 to 8 s. A picture is 100 KB. From the push that starts a
run to the first picture 204 s without a build and 361 s with one when a Mac is free; that night the queue added up to 13 minutes. A run that is cancelled brings its record home within 20 s.
