## Seen on the way

Not among the fifteen; for the lab and for the board's keeper.

1. **`replay --quiet` hides refusals.** `REFUSED:` is printed only when `--quiet` is absent (`Sources/arbor-sim/ChaosModes.swift:136`), and so is `# every invariant held` (`:151`). A quiet replay of a script with an unknown word looks like a clean one, which is how K1990 was once "not reproduced". **Mended** in the pull request this report comes with: refusals show in a quiet replay too.
2. **`way-back start-again` breaks `home-leaves-out` on a desk where nothing is wrong**: two docked windows, one framed, nobody hangs (`control-start-again.txt`, ten lines): `after start-again, w102 is still out of view, at (1439,868 464×851)`. The start brought back the framed view there was (`control-crash-launch.txt`: `camera n1  hidden: w102`; `home` then fetches it). The rule is not among the known failures. So the same line in K003's `start-again` says nothing about K003. **Mended since**: `start-again` is asked only of the displays whose view is on everything.
3. **The sibling of K2036 that the release notes print at 3 is as they say, and heavier at its worst** (`docs/RELEASE-NOTES.md`, "A display that changes side and gets smaller in one step, apps reporting first"). `k2036-sibling-p.txt`: a display 2560×1440 on the left becomes 1280×720 on the right, `windows-first`: `w110 at (2668,609 700×400)`, 7 % showing. Arbor makes no move: the lab's macOS left it there, and in the usual order Arbor fits it ((2020,609), `k2036-sibling-p-late.txt`). Each alone (`k2036-sibling-p-way-*.txt`): ⌃⌥0, Show Everything, Float All, ⌃⌥D, Lay Out Again, turning to it, starting again: NO; pause: yes (740,500); quit: yes (740,500); docking it: yes. No net speaks, not even after `way-back home`. It rests on two unmeasured things at once: that order of reports, and the lab's idea of where macOS leaves such a window.
4. Rules that broke in these replays and are the lab's known failures, none of them the row's fault: `fetched-before-any-dock` (the scripts of K1774 and K1990 (B) open a window off the display before `launch`), `size-kept-through-a-pause` (every `pause` with something docked), `left-its-place` (K1989's eight points), `moved-twice-sizing`, `app-switch-out-of-view` (`switch-app` alone leaves a window that is out of view where it is: 24 of K1774's 288, each still on Arbor's list, each fetched by `home`).
5. **Float All while an app hangs is not made up for in size** (`k059.txt`): the hung app's three windows stay in view at their slots' sizes ((12,469 557×419) and so on) after `wake` and `settle`; their own 600×400 does not come back. Nothing is lost; of the kind of the lab's rule 5.

Smaller than K1770: nobody is in the middle of an install there, and what Arbor does when the permission is taken away has a spec of its own by now (F63).
