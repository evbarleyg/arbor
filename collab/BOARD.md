Letters name the lane that has an item. "Archive" is the branch of patches issue #4 speaks of: a patch comes over only after every added line has been read, through one hand, after an automatic privacy scan. **free** means nobody has it: take it, and tell the coordinator.

## 0. What the first days of use said: less by default

The maintainer worked with the first build for a session, **then quit it and went back to macOS's own tiling: for how they work today, that is better than Arbor.** Arbor does too much by itself, has too many shortcuts, and gets in the way. What they said is `collab/IDEAS.md`, I16 to I25, and `collab/HANDOFF-2026-10-14-maintainer.md`; it is ruled on in `collab/QUESTIONS.md`, Q29. Arbor does nothing that was not asked for, and what was asked for touches only the windows it names.

**The bar is no longer the reference. It is macOS alone**: on every journey Arbor is no worse than macOS's own tiling, and on what Arbor is for it is better.
