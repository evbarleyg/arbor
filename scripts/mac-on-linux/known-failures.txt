# A window closes and "destroyed" never comes. The layer reads an app's list only when a window is created, the focused or main window changes, a window comes out of
# the Dock, the app is shown, or the Space changes; and the focus report of a closing window comes 12 ms BEFORE the window goes (trace `close`), when the list
# still has it. After that nothing looks. `setFrame` of such a window gets `invalidUIElement`, reads no frame back and answers nil, for ever.
a window closes and the notification never comes: it is reported gone all the same, once # nothing reads the app's list again, and setFrame does not take invalidUIElement for an answer

a docked window closes and the notification never comes: its neighbour gets the room # the same, seen from the screen
a docked window closes and the notification never comes: at the latest after the person has gone on working # zoom and show-all ask the ghost for a frame, get nil, and keep it

# An app ends and nothing says so (one started from a shell). The layer finds out when a call to it fails, and nothing calls until a layout wants its window moved.
