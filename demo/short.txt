# The short film (demo/STORYBOARD.md): two keys and an arrow, twice; the gap; fill the screen and back; where to find it. A script for `arbor-sim film` (docs/FILM.md).
# It plays Arbor as it comes: no setting is changed.
#
# Every line is either what a person does (pointer, button, keys), what another app does, or a note of the film's own (caption, shot,
# mark). What the windows do in answer is Arbor's business: nothing here says where a window goes once Arbor has it.
# The `expect` lines hold the story to the truth: if Arbor stops doing what a caption says, there is no film.

display 1440x810 bar 24 dock 76
reserve 196

# The colour of each app is what Arbor takes from its icon (demo/render/icons.mjs writes the icons down as Arbor samples them).
app Browser icon icons/Browser.rgba
app Mail icon icons/Mail.rgba
app Notes icon icons/Notes.rgba
app Chat icon icons/Chat.rgba

# ---- The heap. These windows were open before Arbor was. Notes and Chat lie at the back, mostly buried, and where no gap will come to lie:
# a loose window behind two docked ones shows through the gap between them, and a press there is a press on it.
open Browser 820x520
open Mail 640x440
open Notes 380x270
open Chat 420x330
title 101 How a tree grows
title 102 Inbox

title 104 garden
move 101 60 44 820 520
move 102 540 120 640 440
move 103 96 372 380 270
move 104 968 316 420 330
focus 103
focus 104
focus 101
focus 102

shot 1
caption Too many windows. One screen.
wait 0.8
glide window 103 visible
click
wait 0.5
glide window 101 visible
click
wait 0.6

shot 2
caption Meet Arbor. Nothing moves until you say so.
wait 0.4
launch
mark look item
wait 2.5
mark look
mark still.start 101 102 103 104
wait 0.1
mark still.start

shot 3
caption One shortcut. Snapped left.
wait 1.6
tap ctrl+opt+left
expect docked 101
wait 1.9

shot 4
caption One more. Snapped right.
glide window 102 visible
click
wait 0.7
tap ctrl+opt+right
expect docked 102
expect left-of 101 102
expect floating 103
expect floating 104
wait 0.4
# Two halves, and what was left alone has not been touched.
mark alike 101 102
mark still.start 103 104
wait 1.3

# Every viewer asked where the other two had gone.
shot 4b
caption Nothing closed. The rest wait behind.
wait 2.6
mark alike

shot 5
caption Drag the gap. Both windows resize.
glide gap 101 102 in 0.8
wait 0.5
press
glide 872 379 in 1.1
wait 0.4
lift
# Not halves any more: an arrangement of this person's own.
mark wider 101 102
mark keep.mine on
glide 1250 768 in 0.7
wait 0.8
mark wider
# While one window fills the screen Arbor still takes the loose ones out of view, behind it, and puts them back: nothing of that is seen,
# but untouched they are not. From here on the claim is that they are where they were.
mark still.start

shot 6
caption Need room? Fill the screen.
wait 1.6
tap ctrl+opt+return
expect fills 102
wait 2.0

shot 7a
caption Hit the same keys…
wait 1.5
tap ctrl+opt+return
expect camera root
wait 0.9

shot 7b
caption …and your layout is right back.
mark same.mine 101 102
mark same.start 103 104
wait 2.8
